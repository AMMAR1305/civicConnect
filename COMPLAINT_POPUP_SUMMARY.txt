Dashboard, a beautiful modal popup appears showing:

✨ Complete Complaint Information:
   • Title & Full Description
   • Status Badge (color-coded with icon)
   • Priority Level (High/Medium/Low)
   • Category & Location Details
   • Attached Images (if any)
   • Complete Timeline (Created, Updated, SLA)
   • Citizen Information (with avatar)
   • Assigned Officer Details (or "Unassigned" warning)
   • Additional Metadata (Ward, Pincode, Feedback)

✨ Professional UI Features:
   • Blur backdrop effect (backdrop-filter: blur(8px))
   • Click outside to close
   • Background becomes non-clickable
   • Smooth animations (slide-in + scale)
   • Color-coded status indicators
   • Gradient info cards
   • Responsive design (mobile & desktop)
   • Action buttons (Edit, Reassign, Delete)

🎯 HOW TO USE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Navigate to Admin Dashboard
2. Go to "Complaints" tab
3. See complaint cards displayed in grid
4. Click "View Details" button OR click anywhere on card
5. Popup opens with full complaint details
6. Background blurs and becomes non-clickable
7. Scroll to view all information
8. Click X or click outside to close
9. Popup closes with smooth animation
